# Innovation Exploration

Innovation exploration begins only after a reliable performance improvement has been validated.

## First analyze the successful change

Determine:

- whether the gain replicates;
- which samples/classes benefited;
- what regressed;
- whether compute changed materially;
- whether the originally proposed mechanism is actually supported;
- what bottleneck is now most important.

## Innovation candidate requirements

For each direction report:

- target bottleneck;
- project evidence;
- literature gap or limitation;
- relationship to existing methods;
- proposed mechanism;
- concrete experiment;
- implementation complexity;
- compute requirement;
- expected performance effect;
- evidence-grounded confidence;
- major uncertainty;
- what result would falsify the idea.

Do not claim novelty simply because a combination has not been seen in the small set of papers reviewed. A novelty claim requires a dedicated literature check.

It is valid to conclude that current evidence supports engineering optimization but not a defensible research innovation.
